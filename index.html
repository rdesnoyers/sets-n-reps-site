Common questions

Where is my data stored?

In a database on your iPhone, and nowhere else. Sets n Reps has no accounts and makes no network requests at all, so there is no server copy of your training history — not even one I could look at.

How do I back up my workouts?

Open Profile → Backup → Export backup. That writes your whole history to a single file and hands it to the iOS share sheet, so you can keep it in Files, iCloud Drive, or anywhere else you like. The app never uploads it for you.

How do I move to a new phone?

Export a backup on the old phone, then on the new one open Profile → Backup → Restore from backup and pick that file. Restoring replaces everything currently in the app, so export first if there is anything on the new phone you want to keep.

I deleted the app. Can I get my history back?

Only from a backup file you exported yourself. Deleting the app deletes its database, and because nothing is ever uploaded there is no copy for me to restore. This is the trade-off that comes with the app not touching the network — worth exporting a backup every so often.

Can I switch between kilograms and pounds?

Yes, under Profile → Units. Weights are stored once and converted only for display, so switching back and forth never changes what you actually lifted. Changing units also resets the smallest weight jump, since 2.5 is a sensible step in kg and a strange one in lb.

Where does the suggested weight come from?

From the sets you have already logged. The app estimates your one-rep max with the formula you pick — Epley, Brzycki, Lombardi, McGlothin or Wathan — then works backwards to a weight for your target rep range, rounded down to the smallest plate jump you own so the first set lands inside the range instead of above it.

Is it free?

Free for three months or thirty workouts, whichever comes first. After that it is a single purchase — no subscription. Your training history stays readable either way; the app never holds your data hostage.
