Sets n Reps

Privacy Policy

Last updated: 10 September 2026

The short version

Sets n Reps does not collect, transmit, or store any of your personal information. There are no accounts, no analytics, no advertising, and no third-party trackers. The app makes no network requests.

Everything you log stays on your device.

What the app stores

Sets n Reps saves the following in a database on your device, and nowhere else:

  • Your workouts, exercises, sets, weights and reps
  • Your saved routines
  • Your display name and settings (units, plate increment, formula, rest timer)

An exported backup file contains this same information, in plain text.

This data never leaves your phone. It is not backed up to our servers, because there are no servers. We cannot see it, and we could not retrieve it for you even if you asked.

What we collect

Nothing.

  • No account. There is no sign-up, no login, no email address.
  • No analytics. We do not measure how you use the app.
  • No advertising. There are no ads and no advertising identifiers.
  • No third-party SDKs that collect data.
  • No location, contacts, photos, health data, or device identifiers.

Purchases

If you buy the full version, the purchase is processed entirely by Apple. We never see your name, email address, or payment details. Apple tells the app only whether a valid purchase exists, so it knows to unlock. Apple's handling of that transaction is covered by Apple's Privacy Policy.

Deleting your data

Deleting the app removes all of it from your device permanently. Because there is no copy on any server, we cannot restore it for you.

Before that happens you can export everything to a single file from Profile → Backup. The file is handed to the iOS share sheet, so where it ends up — Files, iCloud Drive, AirDrop, somewhere else — is entirely your choice; the app never uploads it. Restoring that file from the same screen brings your history back.

You can also clear your training history from within the app, under Profile → Data.

Children

The app collects no data from anyone, including children under 13.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted at this address with a new date at the top. Since the app collects nothing, we do not expect substantive changes.
